# Design

This file is the canonical design-system backbone for the reference Flutter validation app.

<!-- cbx:design:foundation:start version=1 profile=habit-tracker-app -->
## Visual Direction

- Editorial calm with a premium wellness tone rather than bright productivity-gamification.
- Warm mineral neutrals and deep teal anchors instead of generic wellness gradients.
- Rounded, elevated cards with quiet spacing rhythm and strong section framing.

## Token Language

- `surface/base`: warm paper backgrounds for primary screens
- `surface/card`: brighter shells for cards and drawers
- `ink/primary`: deep blue-green body copy and headings
- `accent/primary`: focused teal for completion, confidence, and flow
- `accent/warm`: clay for quotes, warmth, and secondary highlights

## Typography

- Large editorial headlines for screen anchors
- Strong title weight for section headers and cards
- Compact body copy with enough leading to feel calm, not compressed

## Component Vocabulary

- Hero metric card
- Soft elevated routine cards
- Motivational editorial quote panel
- Section headers with small utility actions
- Bottom navigation with restrained icon emphasis

## Motion

- Keep transitions short and purposeful
- Use feedback motion for toggles and route changes only
- Respect reduced-motion expectations; no decorative looping animation

## Mobile Adaptation

- Primary actions must stay thumb-reachable
- Section stacks should stay readable in one-handed scroll contexts
- Progress visuals should remain glanceable without occupying the entire screen
