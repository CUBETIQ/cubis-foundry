# Neo Market Brief

- Goal: benchmark whether Foundry can express a creator marketplace and fulfillment workspace without collapsing into the scenario's banned defaults.
- Style reference: `neo-brutalism-light-13`
- Direction: `neo-brutalist-product`
- Motif: `brutalist-sticker-stack`
- Layout: `market-grid-cabinet`
- Complexity tier: `complex`
- Anti-slop constraints:
  - avoid brutalism as pure novelty styling
  - avoid offset cards with no transaction logic
  - avoid marketplace UIs that look like generic ecommerce grids
- Required interactions:
  - switch the creator category
  - pin the current order
