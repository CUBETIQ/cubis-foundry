# Maison Prive Brief

- Goal: benchmark whether Foundry can express a luxury concierge and reservation studio without collapsing into the scenario's banned defaults.
- Style reference: `luxury-light-06`
- Direction: `luxury-formal`
- Motif: `concierge-itinerary-stack`
- Layout: `concierge-salon-split`
- Complexity tier: `complex`
- Anti-slop constraints:
  - avoid spa-beige generic luxury
  - avoid rounded ecommerce widgets
  - avoid decorative service cards with no itinerary logic
- Required interactions:
  - switch the residence in focus
  - prepare a concierge itinerary
