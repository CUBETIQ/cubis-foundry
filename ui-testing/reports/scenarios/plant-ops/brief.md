# Plant Ops Brief

- Goal: benchmark whether Foundry can express a manufacturing operations and maintenance dashboard without collapsing into the scenario's banned defaults.
- Style reference: `industrial-light-26`
- Direction: `industrial-control`
- Motif: `status-beacon-strip`
- Layout: `industrial-twin-board`
- Complexity tier: `complex`
- Anti-slop constraints:
  - avoid generic B2B card stacks
  - avoid safety dashboards that feel like a marketing BI tool
  - avoid warning color overload with no hierarchy
- Required interactions:
  - switch the active production line
  - place a line into maintenance hold
