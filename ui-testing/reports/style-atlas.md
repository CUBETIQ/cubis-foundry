# Style Atlas

This atlas is a required supplementary harness artifact.

## Purpose

- compare component geometry across style families
- verify rounded and tactile systems are represented, not just hard-edge shells
- make texture fallback and component-kit reuse visible without relying only on page-level mocks

## Included style lanes

- monochrome editorial
- SaaS minimal
- material expressive
- luxury formal
- neo brutalist product
- terminal ops

## Review focus

- radius and corner behavior
- chip, button, and input language
- card, sheet, and metric treatment
- whether background texture is justified by the style family
