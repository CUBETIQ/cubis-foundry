# XYZ Ecommerce Design System

## Product direction

XYZ Ecommerce is a premium buyer marketplace for fashion and accessories. The product should feel editorial, calm, and expensive without becoming cold or inaccessible.

## Visual language

- Typography: serif display headlines paired with clean modern body text
- Hierarchy: large image-led modules, compact metadata, deliberate whitespace
- Palette: plum, blush, shell, muted gold, deep ink
- Tone: styled magazine spread translated into a usable commerce product

## Token language

- Primary action: berry
- Secondary highlight: gold
- Background shell: warm bone
- Surface card: lifted ivory
- Text primary: deep ink
- Soft accent wash: blush

## Component vocabulary

- editorial hero card
- category chips
- merchandise card with soft radius
- compact order summary
- elevated product detail sheet
- understated settings rows

## Commerce UX rules

- imagery leads every product story
- price hierarchy stays obvious without feeling discount-first
- filters should feel lightweight, not enterprise-heavy
- checkout should be short, readable, and confidence-building
- wishlist and recent views help comparison without creating clutter

## Motion and interaction

- subtle elevation and color shift only
- no noisy bounce or novelty motion
- tab changes and detail pushes should feel quick and calm

## Mobile adaptation

- thumb-friendly bottom navigation
- large tap targets on quantity and wishlist actions
- persistent cart visibility through badge count
- forms should remain short and stacked vertically
