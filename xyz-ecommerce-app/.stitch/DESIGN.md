# XYZ Ecommerce Stitch Mirror

Use the canonical design system from `docs/foundation/DESIGN.md` and these screen intents:

- onboarding: editorial introduction to a premium fashion marketplace
- login: clean buyer auth gate
- home: hero, categories, featured products, recent views
- product detail: image-led product conversion surface
- cart and checkout: calm, premium conversion flow
- orders/profile: archival order history plus buyer settings

Stylistic constraints:

- fashion-editorial typography
- premium but usable merchandising
- strong image hierarchy
- restrained motion
- clear size, color, price, and status language
